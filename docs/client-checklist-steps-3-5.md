# Ishaputra Botanicals: what we need from you (Steps 3 to 5)

The shop is built and runs on our preview. To put it on your own domain and take real orders, we need the items below. Tick them off as you send them. Never send passwords or secret keys in plain email or WhatsApp; we'll share a secure way to hand them over.

## Step 3: your accounts and real content

### Business details (needed before launch)
- [ ] Legal business name
- [ ] Shop address, phone number, email and opening hours (the site shows dummy ones now)
- [ ] WhatsApp number for customer enquiries
- [ ] GSTIN (it prints on every invoice)
- [ ] Your domain name (for example ishaputra.com), and who manages it
- [ ] Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), if you want them on the site
- [ ] Vector logo file (SVG or AI), if you have one

### Accounts to create or share
- [ ] **Razorpay:** a business account. Send the **test keys** first, and the live keys at launch.
- [ ] **Cloudflare:** an account for product images (free tier is enough to start).
- [ ] **WhatsApp Business (Meta):** a Meta business account and a phone number not used on the WhatsApp app. We set up the message templates (login code, order updates).
- [ ] **Google sign-in screen:** the name, logo and support email customers see when they sign in with Google.

### Real products
- [ ] Product list with names, prices, descriptions, ingredients and stock
- [ ] Real product photos (the current ones are AI demo photos with made-up label text)
- [ ] One photo per category (Powders and Shilajit share one now)
- [ ] Confirm the categories: Energy & Vitality, Hair Care, Skin Care, Personal Care, Ayurvedic Essentials, and their sub-categories
- [ ] Which products are really "New arrivals"

### Decisions on the values we filled in for you
Everything below is a placeholder. The site works with it, but nothing goes live until you confirm or change it.

- [ ] **GST:** the rate for each product type (Ayurvedic medicines and cosmetics differ), whether your prices include GST, and whether the bill shows a GST line.
- [ ] **Ships from:** Telangana. Delivery charges for your home state and other states, and the free-delivery amount.
- [ ] **Returns:** a 7-day return window, and the return and replacement wording on the policy pages.
- [ ] **Loyalty points:** points per ₹100, what a point is worth, days before points can be used, and the maximum share of a bill that points can pay.
- [ ] **Loyalty tiers:** Silver from ₹0 (1x points), Gold from ₹5,000 (1.25x), Platinum from ₹15,000 (1.5x), Premium from ₹30,000 (2x). The spend counts money paid online on delivered orders. **Please confirm these before launch:** the multipliers apply to every existing customer as soon as the site goes live.
- [ ] **Scratch & Win** (off until you say yes): one card per delivered online order of ₹499 or more; prizes of 5, 10, 25 or 50 points; cards expire 30 days after delivery. Tell us your monthly budget for prizes.
- [ ] **Dealer membership** (off until you say yes): shops and salons apply on the site and you approve them. Approved dealers get 20% off on orders worth ₹2,000 or more at regular prices. Dealer orders don't take coupons or points and don't earn points. Confirm the discount and the minimum, whether some products need their own dealer price, and which business types can apply.
- [ ] **Claims on the site** to confirm or remove: "24 h support" / "reply within 24 hours", "100% secure", "insured delivery", "wellness experts", "video consultation with an expert".
- [ ] **Testimonials:** send real customer reviews (with permission), or we remove the 3 demo ones.
