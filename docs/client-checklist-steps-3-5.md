### Decisions on the values we filled in for you
Everything below is a sample value. The site works with it, but nothing goes live until you confirm or change it.

- [ ] **GST:** the rate for each product type (Ayurvedic medicines and cosmetics can differ), whether your prices include GST, and whether the bill shows a GST line.
- [ ] **Delivery:** you ship from Telangana. Tell us the delivery charge inside Telangana, the charge for other states, and the order value above which delivery is free.
- [ ] **Returns:** a 7-day return window. Please read the return, replacement, shipping, privacy and terms pages and tell us what to change.
- [ ] **Loyalty points:** how many points per ₹100 spent, what one point is worth, how many days before new points can be used, and the most of a bill that points can pay.
- [ ] **Loyalty levels:** Silver from ₹0 (normal points), Gold from ₹5,000 (1.25x points), Platinum from ₹15,000 (1.5x), Premium from ₹30,000 (2x). The spend counts what a customer paid online on delivered orders. Confirm the names, amounts and multipliers, and whether any level should give something extra. **Please confirm before launch:** the levels apply to every existing customer as soon as the site goes live.
- [ ] **Scratch & Win** (switched off until you say yes): one scratch card per delivered online order of ₹499 or more; prizes of 5, 10, 25 or 50 points; cards expire 30 days after delivery. Right now every card wins something (about 13 points a card on average). Tell us your monthly prize budget and whether some cards should win nothing.
- [ ] **Dealer membership** (switched off until you say yes): shops and salons apply on the site and you approve them. Approved dealers get 20% off orders worth ₹2,000 or more. Dealer orders can't use coupons or points and don't earn points. Confirm the discount and the minimum, whether some products need their own dealer price, which business types can apply (retail shop, Ayurvedic store, salon, gym, online seller, distributor, other), and the wording on the dealer page.
- [ ] **Claims on the site** to confirm or remove: "24 h support" / "reply within 24 hours", "100% secure", "insured delivery", "wellness experts", "video consultation with an expert".
- [ ] **Customer reviews:** send real customer reviews (with their permission), or we remove the 3 sample ones.
